About Us

Zahira Spa Consulting & Training

Zahira was born from rapid industry growth and a lack of formal training or professional consulting for spa, salon and skin-care businesses. With years of hands-on experience, we deliver practical training, tailored consulting, and hands-on support to help spa owners and professionals succeed.

Our services include in-house training, operational audits, menu development, customer experience design, and business coaching — all delivered by industry-experienced professionals.
